The gamification system has four core mechanics: streak, XP, levels, and badges.

9 milestone badges with specific unlock conditions:
- First Step: first correct answer
- Kindling: 5 correct in a row (streak milestone)
- Steady Flame: 10 correct in a row
- Unbroken: 20 correct in a row
- Diligent: 25 total correct
- Devoted: 50 total correct
- Halfway: 36 of 71 cards seen
- Complete Path: all 71 cards seen
- Flawless: 100% accuracy for the session

Persistence via localStorage auto-save (no buttons, no prompts). Persist: XP total, current level, best streak, earned badges. Do NOT persist: deck position, session score.

Each user's data is stored only in their own browser — separate and independent per device.

Tone: calm and thematic — gold on dark temple aesthetic. Serene language.
