The gamification system has four core mechanics: streak, XP, levels, and badges.

Streak: consecutive correct answers. Resets on wrong answer. Calm flame icon that glows as the streak grows. Displays current and session-best streak.

XP system: 10 XP per correct answer. 9 thematic level names: Seeker through Enlightened. Level ring and XP progress bar shown in sidebar.

Badges: 9 milestone achievements earned during the session. Toast notification appears when each badge is unlocked.

Persistence via localStorage auto-save after every answer. Persist: XP total, current level, best streak, earned badges. Do NOT persist: deck position or session score.

Each user's data is stored only in their own browser — separate and independent per device.

Tone: calm and thematic — gold on dark temple aesthetic. Serene language. No confetti or sounds.
