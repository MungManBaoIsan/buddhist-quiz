The app has three difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard.

Easy difficulty:
- Timer: 60 seconds per question (Multiple Choice and Fill-in-Blank only)
- Distractors: cross-chapter

Medium difficulty:
- Timer: 40 seconds per question
- Distractors: hand-crafted plausible options and negative questions

Hard difficulty:
- Timer: 24 seconds per question (MC and FIB only)
- Distractors: hand-crafted plausible options and negative questions
- Timer expiry: marks card for review, reveals correct answer, auto-advances

Flashcard mode is always untimed — timer applies to MC and FIB modes only, never Flashcard mode.
