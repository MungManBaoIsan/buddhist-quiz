The app has three difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard.

Easy difficulty:
- Timer: 60 seconds per question (Multiple Choice and Fill-in-Blank only)
- Multiple Choice distractors: cross-chapter (easy to eliminate by topic)
- Flashcard mode: untimed — always

Medium difficulty:
- Timer: 40 seconds per question (MC and FIB only)
- Distractors: hand-crafted plausible options and negative questions

Hard difficulty:
- Timer: 24 seconds per question (MC and FIB only)
- Distractors: hand-crafted plausible options and negative questions

Flashcard mode is untimed across all difficulty levels — it is for reading and absorbing, not testing under pressure.
